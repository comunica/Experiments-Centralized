# Results of February 2025

Runs of the WatDiv (scales 10 and 100) and BSBM (1,000, 10,000 and 100,000 products) experiments with Comunica v4, which were shown on https://comunica.dev/performance/ until November 2026.

Each directory has the query times per template, split into plots of the cheaper (`plot_small`) and more expensive templates (`plot_large`, and `plot_medium` for WatDiv 100), as CSV, TeX and SVG files.
